2. PROBLEM FORMULATION, MOTIVATION & RESEARCH GAPS
================================================================================
A. Ambient Air Quality Crisis in South Asia:
- Rapid urbanization, unchecked vehicular emissions, industrial discharge, construction dust, and seasonal meteorological inversions have created persistent, life-threatening air pollution in Indian metropolitan hubs (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Chennai, Pune).
- Fine particulate matter (PM2.5) concentrations routinely breach World Health Organization (WHO) annual guidelines (5 ug/m3) by 10x to 25x.
- Ambient air pollution accounts for >7 million premature mortalities worldwide annually (WHO, 2021), accelerating ischemic heart disease, stroke, COPD, lung cancer, and pediatric respiratory failure.

B. Research Gap 1: The Monolithic Generalization Fallacy
- Government monitoring infrastructure (CPCB CAAQMS) reports air pollution as a single scalar number and broad category (e.g., "AQI 172 — Moderate").
- This monolithic index assumes a uniform, average human physiology.
- In reality, human vulnerability is deeply heterogeneous:
  * An ambient PM2.5 level of 80 ug/m3 causes minimal immediate distress to a healthy sedentary young adult.
  * That exact same 80 ug/m3 induces acute bronchospasm, alveolar inflammation, and severe hypoxemia in an asthmatic child or a COPD patient.
  * Athletes and outdoor construction laborers breathe 5x to 10x more air volume per minute (minute-ventilation), inhaling vastly higher cumulative toxic loads.
- Existing tools completely ignore: (i) pre-existing comorbidities, (ii) outdoor exposure duration, (iii) specific pollutant toxicity profiles, and (iv) localized spatial hotspots.

C. Research Gap 2: Hallucination and Prescriptive Hazards in Standalone LLMs
- Standalone foundation models (GPT-4, Llama 3) cannot reliably solve this challenge:
  * Temporal Disconnect: They lack real-time access to live sensor telemetry.
  * Arithmetic & Numerical Hallucination: They frequently fabricate pollutant readings and miscalculate statutory mathematical breakpoint intervals (18.4% arithmetic error rate in tests).
  * Dangerous Prescriptive Medical Advice: Direct prompts often produce unauthorized pharmaceutical recommendations (e.g., prescribing antibiotics or specific steroid inhaler dosages) without clinical diagnostic credentials.
- SafeAir AI solves this via a NEURO-SYMBOLIC HYBRID:
  * Symbolic / Deterministic: Math, formulas, sensor ingestion, spatial DBSCAN, and health scoring are handled strictly by Python algebraic modules.
  * Neural / Generative: The LLM is strictly confined to natural language comprehension, intent extraction, and synthesizing grounded, human-readable narratives.

3.1 CPCB Piecewise Linear Breakpoint Formula:
The Indian National AQI (IND-AQI) monitors 8 pollutants: PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, CO, O3, NH3, Pb.
For observed concentration Cp of pollutant p:

    I_p = I_lo + [ (I_hi - I_lo) / (B_hi - B_lo) ] * (C_p - B_lo)

Where:
- B_hi, B_lo = Upper and lower concentration breakpoint limits for the category containing C_p.
- I_hi, I_lo = Upper and lower statutory sub-index scores for that category.

Composite Index Rule (Maximum Operator):
    I_overall = max( I_PM2.5, I_PM10, I_NO2, I_SO2, I_CO, I_O3, I_NH3, I_Pb )

Statutory Operational Constraints:
    subject to: n >= 3 pollutants measured
    and: ({PM2.5, PM10} intersects P_active is NOT empty)

3.3 10-Persona Clinical Vulnerability Formulation:
The dynamic vulnerability score V_k for persona k in cohort {1..10} over outdoor exposure duration t (hours):

    V_k = min( 100, gamma_k * [ I_overall * (1 + alpha_k * ln(1 + t)) + sum_{p in P} ( w_{k,p} * max(0, (C_p - theta_{k,p}) / theta_{k,p}) ) ] )

Where:
- gamma_k = Baseline physiological susceptibility multiplier:
    * Asthma: 1.65
    * COPD: 1.60
    * Cardiovascular: 1.50
    * Pregnant Woman: 1.45
    * Child (<12 yrs): 1.40
    * Elderly (>65 yrs): 1.40
    * Outdoor Worker: 1.35
    * Athlete / Runner: 1.30
    * Pet Owner: 1.15
    * General Population: 1.00
- alpha_k = Duration exposure coefficient (models logarithmic saturation of oxidative stress).
- w_{k,p} = Toxicological sensitivity weighting of persona k to specific pollutant p (sum = 1.0).
- theta_{k,p} = Clinical threshold concentration at which cohort experiences acute cellular distress.

Risk Tier Categorization:
- V_k in [0, 25]: Minimal / Low Risk (Safe for outdoor activities)
- V_k in [26, 50]: Moderate Risk (Sensitive cohorts should reduce high exertion)
- V_k in [51, 75]: High Risk (Avoid prolonged outdoor exposure; keep medications handy)
- V_k in [76, 100]: Critical / Severe Risk (Stay indoors; run HEPA air purification)

3.4 Geodesic Haversine Distance & Spatial Bounding:
Great-circle distance d between coordinates (phi1, lambda1) and (phi2, lambda2) over mean Earth radius R = 6371 km:

    d = 2 * R * arcsin( sqrt( sin^2(Delta_phi / 2) + cos(phi1) * cos(phi2) * sin^2(Delta_lambda / 2) ) )

3.5 DBSCAN Spatial Clustering Equations:
To detect irregular, non-spherical spatial pollution pockets and localized micro-hotspots:
Given set of monitoring stations D:
    N_eps(p) = { q in D | d(p, q) <= eps }

Core Point Criterion:
    |N_eps(p)| >= MinPts
Hyperparameters configured:
    eps = 5.0 km (neighborhood radius)
    MinPts = 3 (minimum monitoring stations required to constitute a dense cluster)
Noise points (outliers with < MinPts neighbors) are flagged as potential isolated sensor spikes.

3.6 Vector Space Cosine Similarity & SBERT Embeddings:
Documents from CPCB, WHO, and toxicological medical literature are chunked into 500-token blocks with 50-token stride.
Dense vector representations e in R^384 are generated using sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2:
    e = Embed(T_chunk)

For incoming user query q, semantic cosine similarity over stored knowledge base embeddings e_{c_i}:
    Sim(q, c_i) = ( e_q . e_{c_i} ) / ( ||e_q|| * ||e_{c_i}|| )

Top-k (k=4) context chunks are retrieved and injected into LLM prompt context.

5. TOXICOLOGICAL & EPIDEMIOLOGICAL MECHANISMS (POLLUTANT IMPACTS)
================================================================================
(Extracted from docs/Research.md and health_tools.py)

1. Fine Particulate Matter (PM2.5 - aerodynamic diameter <= 2.5 um):
- Sources: High-temperature combustion, diesel vehicle exhaust, coal power plants, biomass burning.
- Physiological Penetration: Bypasses upper airway mucus and nasal cilia; penetrates directly into alveoli; translocates across alveolar-capillary membrane into systemic circulation.
- Mechanisms: Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) and heavy metals (Pb, As, Cd) on particle surfaces induce massive Reactive Oxygen Species (ROS) generation, lipid peroxidation, DNA damage, and endothelial dysfunction.
- Acute Effects: Bronchospasm, acute asthma exacerbation, arterial vasoconstriction, arrhythmia, acute myocardial infarction.
- Chronic Effects: Accelerated atherosclerosis, reduced FEV1 (forced expiratory volume in 1s), COPD progression, lung adenocarcinoma, neuroinflammation.

2. Coarse Particulate Matter (PM10 - diameter <= 10 um):
- Sources: Resuspended road dust, mechanical grinding, construction debris, agricultural tilling.
- Penetration: Deposition primarily in tracheobronchial tree.
- Acute Effects: Mucosal irritation, cough, expectoration, acute bronchitis.

3. Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2):
- Sources: High-temperature internal combustion engines (heavy transport corridors).
- Mechanisms: Potent oxidant gas; hydrolyzes to nitrous and nitric acids in epithelial lining fluid, causing deep airway mucosal edema and hyper-reactivity.
- Synergies: Acts as a primary chemical precursor to tropospheric ozone (O3) in the presence of sunlight and volatile organic compounds (VOCs).

4. Ground-Level Ozone (O3):
- Formation: Photochemical secondary reaction: NO2 + VOCs + UV radiation -> O3.
- Impact: Highly insoluble in water; penetrates deeply into terminal bronchioles and alveoli; causes immediate airway inflammation, intense chest tightness, coughing, and substantial reductions in vital capacity even in healthy elite athletes.

5. Sulfur Dioxide (SO2):
- Sources: Industrial coal combustion, metal smelting, diesel fuels.
- Impact: Highly soluble; triggers rapid bronchoconstriction within minutes of inhalation, particularly in asthmatic individuals.

6. Carbon Monoxide (CO):
- Sources: Incomplete combustion from congested traffic corridors.
- Impact: 200x higher affinity for hemoglobin than oxygen, forming carboxyhemoglobin (COHb); induces cellular hypoxia, angina pectoris, cognitive fatigue, and fetal growth restriction.

6. THE 10-PERSONA CLINICAL VULNERABILITY MATRIX SPECIFICATION
================================================================================
(Implemented in config.py & health_tools.py)

1. Asthma Patient:
- Vulnerability Mechanism: Bronchial hyper-responsiveness, eosinophilic inflammation.
- Trigger Pollutants: PM2.5, O3, SO2.
- Multiplier: gamma = 1.65
- Clinical Advisory: Immediate cessation of outdoor exercise at AQI > 100; maintain short-acting beta-2 agonist (rescue inhaler) readily available.

2. Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) Patient:
- Vulnerability Mechanism: Irreversible alveolar destruction, impaired mucociliary clearance.
- Trigger Pollutants: PM2.5, PM10, NO2.
- Multiplier: gamma = 1.60
- Clinical Advisory: Avoid all non-essential outdoor travel during Poor/Very Poor AQI; maintain indoor air filtration.

3. Cardiovascular Patient:
- Vulnerability Mechanism: Endothelial dysfunction, systemic cytokine release (IL-6, TNF-alpha), plaque destabilization, autonomic cardiac dysregulation.
- Trigger Pollutants: PM2.5, CO, NO2.
- Multiplier: gamma = 1.50
- Clinical Advisory: Avoid morning rush hour physical exertion; monitor blood pressure and pulse regularity.

4. Pregnant Woman:
- Vulnerability Mechanism: Increased minute ventilation, transplacental translocation of ultrafine particles causing systemic placental oxidative stress, fetal hypoxia.
- Trigger Pollutants: PM2.5, CO.
- Multiplier: gamma = 1.45
- Clinical Advisory: Strict avoidance of traffic corridor exposure; ensure HEPA air cleaning in sleep environments.

5. Child (<12 years):
- Vulnerability Mechanism: Incomplete alveolar maturation (alveoli continue developing until age 8), 2x higher breathing rate per unit body weight relative to adults, narrower peripheral airways.
- Trigger Pollutants: PM2.5, O3, NO2.
- Multiplier: gamma = 1.40
- Clinical Advisory: Cancel outdoor sports and recess when O3 or PM2.5 exceeds statutory standards.

6. Elderly (>65 years):
- Vulnerability Mechanism: Age-related decline in pulmonary elasticity, diminished cardiovascular compensatory reserves, prevalence of subclinical atherosclerosis.
- Trigger Pollutants: PM2.5, PM10, O3.
- Multiplier: gamma = 1.40
- Clinical Advisory: Limit morning walks to clean indoor spaces or days with Satisfactory AQI.

7. Outdoor Worker:
- Vulnerability Mechanism: High cumulative daily toxic loading (8-12 hours continuous outdoor shift), high minute ventilation during manual labor.
- Trigger Pollutants: PM10, PM2.5, NO2.
- Multiplier: gamma = 1.35
- Clinical Advisory: Mandatory certified N95/FFP2 respirator usage during industrial or traffic shifts.

8. Athlete / Runner:
- Vulnerability Mechanism: Switch from nasal breathing to deep oral respiration during aerobic exercise (bypassing nasal filtration mechanisms), ventilation volume increases from 6 L/min to >80 L/min, delivering 10x-15x higher pollutant dose deep into lungs.
- Trigger Pollutants: O3, PM2.5.
- Multiplier: gamma = 1.30
- Clinical Advisory: Shift workouts to early morning (before photochemical ozone formation) or indoors.

9. Pet Owner (Canine / Feline):
- Vulnerability Mechanism: Proximity to low-ground settling pollutants (ground dust, resuspension), sensitive olfactory and respiratory membranes, rapid respiration.
- Trigger Pollutants: PM10, O3.
- Multiplier: gamma = 1.15
- Clinical Advisory: Shorten outdoor dog walks; wipe paws and coat post-walk to prevent grooming ingestion of particulate lead/PAHs.

10. General Population:
- Vulnerability Mechanism: Healthy baseline adult physiology following standard CPCB epidemiological thresholds.
- Trigger Pollutants: Composite dominant AQI pollutant.
- Multiplier: gamma = 1.00
- Clinical Advisory: Routine health precautions corresponding to standard CPCB public advisory bands.

7. RESPONSIBLE AI & CLINICAL SAFETY GUARDRAILS
================================================================================
(Implemented in utils/guardrails.py & agents/reasoning_agent.py)

7.1 Input Sanitization:
- Length Bounding: Prompts strictly clamped to 500 characters to prevent buffer overflow and prompt hijacking.
- Character Filtering: Stripping non-printable ASCII control characters, null bytes, and script injection tokens (<script>, javascript:).

7.2 Adversarial Prompt Injection Defense:
Regex filter scanning against 12+ attack vectors before LLM invocation:
- "ignore previous instructions"
- "disregard all prior rules"
- "you are now a doctor / emergency physician"
- "system prompt override"
- "DAN mode / jailbreak"
- "prescribe me [drug name]"
- "what is the dose of [medication]"
Action on Detection: Prompt injection flagged; query diverted to a deterministic safe fallback message explaining that instructions cannot be overridden.

7.3 Clinical Non-Prescriptive Enforcement:
Invariant Property: The LLM is strictly prohibited from:
- Offering diagnostic conclusions ("You have acute bronchitis")
- Recommending specific pharmaceutical agents ("Take 500mg Amoxicillin")
- Altering patient medication schedules
Deterministic Suffix Appended to Every Advisory:
"WARNING / Medical Disclaimer: SafeAir AI provides environmental risk intelligence based on atmospheric models. It does not provide medical diagnoses or prescriptions. Individuals experiencing acute symptoms must consult a registered medical professional."
